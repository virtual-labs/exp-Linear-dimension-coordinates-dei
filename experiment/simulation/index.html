Example 1a: S = {(1, 1), (1, 2)}

Guess linear independence of S. to check.


S is LINEARLY INDEPENDENT

b ≡ (1, 2) ∉ L({(1, 1)})

α . (1, 1) + β . (1, 2) = (0, 0)
  ⇒ (α + β , α + 2β) = (0, 0)
  ⇒ α + β = 0 , α + 2β = 0
  ⇒ α = 0 , β = 0
