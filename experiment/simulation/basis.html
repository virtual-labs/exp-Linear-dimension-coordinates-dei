Basis, Dimension and Co-ordinates

NOTE: Consider the vector space R2 over R

I. Example
i. B = {(1, 0), (0, 1)} ⊆ R2
(for facts related to B)

  • B is basis
  • Dim R2 = 2
  • Coordinates of (a, b) are a and b w.r.t. the basis R
  • In particular, coordinates of (1, 2) are 1 and 2 w.r.t. the basis R.

ii. B = {(1, 1), (1, 2)} ⊆ R2
(for facts related to B)

  • B is basis
  • Dim R2 = 2
  • Coordinates of (a, b) are 2a-b and b-a w.r.t. the basis B
  • In particular, coordinates of (1, 2) are 0 and 1 w.r.t. the basis B.
